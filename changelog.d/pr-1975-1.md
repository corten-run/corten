- A link, form or redirect the runtime follows to a `#fragment` now gets the browser's own scroll to it, as a full load does:
  the element matches `:target` and an `<a name>` anchor is found. Firefox still scrolls there, but may leave `:target` unset or stale after its first fragment scroll
  ([#1940 A page the runtime draws at a #fragment never matches :target, and an a name anchor is not scrolled to](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/1940)).
