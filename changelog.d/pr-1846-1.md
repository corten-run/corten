- A kept `Public` page, JSON, text or file answer of up to 32 KiB can now go to browsers in
  brotli 5, made off the request once browsers have been given it often enough to repay the
  encoding (8,192 times and three more a byte). They then get it unless a form already made is no
  larger. Best effort ([A kept page is compressed at the level a per-request answer is](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/1793)).
