- A connection keeps one timer for its requests' deadlines from its second request that waits,
  instead of making one a request, which took a lock all threads share twice a request. Deadlines
  fire when they did; a test on tokio's paused clock that leaves a served connection open can see
  the clock move to that timer's time
  ([cancellation](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/blob/main/docs/guide/cancellation.md#cancellation),
  [#2029 Keep one deadline timer for a connection's requests](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/pull/2029)).
