- A page, patch or JSON answer up to 16 KiB compressed for a client that accepts gzip but not zstd is now
  gzip made by libdeflate: fewer bytes on eleven of twelve bodies measured, for about a third less CPU. Larger
  ones stay brotli, and `compress` now also compiles libdeflate's C sources
  ([#1971 Compress a request's small body with gzip by libdeflate for a client without zstd](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/pull/1971)).
