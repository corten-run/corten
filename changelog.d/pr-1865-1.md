- On a route with stages, a `Public` answer's `Cache-Control` is no longer allocated for each answer or read directive by directive before it goes out: a thread reuses the headers of its last four policies, and one it still holds is sent without that read. Under callgrind that is 2.5% to 5.2% fewer instructions a request on the measured `Public` pages, a count of work rather than of CPU time ([Choice 70](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/blob/main/docs/design.md#choice-kept-pages)). An unstaged route's header, `no-store` and `immutable` are made as before.
  ([#1825 A Public page costs 3,300 instructions more than the same page for one visitor, before anything is kept](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/1825))
