- A `Public` answer's `Cache-Control` costs less: a thread reuses its last four policies' headers, and it is not parsed again while it stands as `Public` wrote it. In process (callgrind, glibc), a `Public` page with `kept_answers(0)` took about 1,050 fewer instructions, a kept hit 380 fewer.
