- A kept page or body holds its headers as a list of their own size, not a copy of the handler's header map, and its `Cache-Control` at its own length, so each answer the store keeps takes less of the process's memory. What counts against `App::kept_answers` and what a visitor receives are unchanged. An answer with more than 1,024 header names is no longer kept: its handler answers every request for it, where one near the most names an `http` header map holds could make a request given it again panic
  ([#1824 A Public page asked for once costs 44% more cycles than the same page not kept](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/1824)).
