- A nav link whose `href` no browser can parse as a URL, such as `//[` from a route implemented by
  hand, no longer stops the runtime from marking the page's other nav links current, at a full
  load or after a client navigation
  ([#2119 A nav entry cannot be current at an older address that serves the same page](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/2119)).
