- A flush or close that waited and then went through, with no write or close waiting, now ends its send-timeout wait, so a later write that waits gets its own `send_timeout` instead of closing at once. Only an app's own IO under `serve_wrapped` could hit this. [#1762 A flush or close that waits and then goes through leaves the send timeout's stall start set, so a later wait may be cut early](https://github.com/corten-run/corten/issues/1762)
